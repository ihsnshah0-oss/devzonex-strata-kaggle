L01 — Strategy 1: Trend Pullback [1% · standard ·
Window A/B]
Edge: uptrends persist; the 20MA is where institutions reload. You buy proven demand, not hope.
Best when: FULL regime, green M3 sector. Downtrend rule: OFF in DEFENSE — pullbacks in
downtrends keep falling (that's S1's market, not yours).
STAGE 1 — PRE-SIGNAL (WATCH) · evening scan
ALL must be true: (1) close above 50MA, 50MA sloping up; (2) 20MA above 50MA; (3) today's low
touched or came within 2% of 20MA, OR price within 3% above 20MA and falling toward it; (4)
RSI(14) 35–60; (5) no results/announcement in 48h; (6) regime FULL or CAUTION. Action:
WATCHLIST it. Pre-write: trigger = close above prior day's high; stop = 3.5% below 20MA; T1 =
prior swing high; size via calculator. Set price alert at prior day's high.
STAGE 2 — BUY TRIGGER · Window A next morning (or B same-day
close)
ALL must be true: (1) daily candle CLOSES above previous day's high (Window B) or trades above
it after 10:00 AM (Window A); (2) candle closes in TOP HALF of its range; (3) volume ≥ 20-day
average; (4) price still within 4% of 20MA (not extended); (5) regime still FULL/CAUTION. Order:
limit at trigger +0.3% (chase cap). Stop placed SAME session. Reasoning: close-above-prior-high
proves sellers failed at the MA and buyers regained control; top-half close + volume proves
demand, not a dead-cat bounce; 4%-from-MA cap guarantees the planned R:R.
Trade frame
Stop 3–4% below 20MA (or pullback low, lower of the two) · T1 prior high = ½ out · trail rest
below 20MA · hold 5–20 days · stale (>10 days, no progress) → exit half.
Invalidation — kill it if: close below 50MA · RSI breaks below 35 and
stays · support/MA zone fails on volume · regime flips DEFENSE (exit,
don't wait for stop if thesis dead).
Example
MCB: 20MA 285, dip to 286, RSI 52 → WATCH. Next day closes 292 above prior high 290 on good
volume → BUY 292. Stop 274 (risk 18). T1 310. Rs 5,000 risk = 277 sh (~Rs 81k).
Don'ts
Never buy mid-plunge before the trigger candle; never widen the stop "to give room".

L02 — Strategy 2: Breakout + Retest [1% · standard ·
Window A/B]
Edge: multi-week resistance = stored breakout energy + trapped shorts fueling the retest
bounce. Retest entry cuts false-breakout losses ~half. Best when: FULL regime only.
Downtrend rule: OFF in DEFENSE and CAUTION — breakouts are the FIRST thing that fails when
the market turns down.
STAGE 1 — PRE-SIGNAL (WATCH) · evening scan
ALL: (1) clean horizontal resistance, 2–3+ touches over 3+ weeks; (2) price within 3% BELOW
resistance and candles shrinking (coiling); (3) volume drying into the squeeze; (4) regime FULL;
(5) green/neutral M3; (6) no announcements 48h. Action: WATCHLIST. Pre-write: breakout level,
retest zone (±1%), stop 3% below level, target = range height. Alert at resistance.
STAGE 2 — BUY TRIGGER · two-step, Window B then A
Step 1 (confirm): daily CLOSE 2%+ above resistance on ≥1.5x average volume. Step 2 (enter):
within 1–5 days price dips back to breakout level ±1% AND prints a bullish candle (close top-half)
→ BUY next morning Window A above that candle's high (or Window B close). Order: limit at
retest zone top. Stop same session. Reasoning: the volume breakout proves supply absorbed;
the HOLDING retest proves old resistance became demand — two independent confirmations
before risking capital. Skipping straight to breakout-chase removes confirmation #2 and most of
the edge. No-retest path (only if +volume ≥2.5x and close top 10% of range): half-size
breakout entry next morning, stop same; still prefer retest.
Trade frame
Stop 3% below level · ½ at +7–8%, trail rest · 2–6 weeks · retest never comes in 5 days → cancel
plan (don't chase extended).
Invalidation: close back BELOW level after entry · breakout volume
<1.2x (weak) · regime flips · broader sector breaks down.
Example
LUCK 950–1000 base → WATCH at 995. Breaks out, closes 1025 (2x vol). Dips to 1005 + bullish
candle → BUY 1008. Stop 975 (risk 33). T1 1060. Rs 5k = 151 sh.
Don'ts
Never buy the first green rocket; never enter breakouts when KSE-100 is below its 50MA.

L03 — Strategy 3: Range Bounce [1% · standard · Window
A/B]
Edge: proven two-sided auction — support has 2+ prints of demand. Buying the floor with a hard
invalidation = best-defined risk in the book. Best when: FULL/CAUTION, stock NOT in strong
trend (that's L01's job). Downtrend rule: OFF in DEFENSE — range floors are trapdoors in falling
markets; existing range longs get stops tightened to breakeven minimum.
STAGE 1 — PRE-SIGNAL (WATCH) · evening scan
ALL: (1) range ≥1 month old, width ≥8–10%; (2) price in BOTTOM 25% of range and falling
toward support; (3) RSI <45 and flattening; (4) support touched 2+ times before with real
bounces; (5) index not in selloff; (6) no announcements 48h. Action: WATCHLIST. Pre-write:
support line, stop 2.5% below it, target = range top minus 2%, size. Alert 1.5% above support.
STAGE 2 — BUY TRIGGER · Window A or B
ALL: (1) price tagged support (±1%) intraday or prior day; (2) bullish candle CLOSES (top-half)
at/above support; (3) RSI hooks up from <40 (or bullish engulfing/hammer regardless); (4)
volume ≥ average on the turn candle; (5) no breakdown close below support. Order: limit at
trigger-candle high +0.3% next morning (A), or close-of-confirmation (B). Reasoning: tag +
bullish close = the third (or fourth) demand print at the level; RSI hook confirms momentum
turned WITH price instead of diverging against it; entering only after the close avoids catching
the knife mid-plunge.
Trade frame
Stop 2–3% below support (break = INSTANT exit, no "one more day") · target range top, sell
70–100% · 1–4 weeks.
Invalidation: ANY daily close 2%+ below support · range visibly
breaking (lower lows forming) · regime DEFENSE.
Example
FFC 100–112: touches 101, RSI 35 hooking, bullish close 103 → BUY 103. Stop 97.5 (risk 5.5).
Target 110. Rs 5k = 909 sh.
Don'ts
Never buy mid-range; never "hope" a broken floor.

L04 — M1: Results-Week Momentum [1% · standard ·
Window A]
Edge: post-earnings drift — institutions take weeks to build on real beats; you ride their
accumulation. Best when: FULL/CAUTION + stock was healthy pre-results. Downtrend rule:
OFF in DEFENSE — even good beats get sold in falling markets ("good news, down price" =
distribution, stand aside).
STAGE 1 — PRE-SIGNAL (WATCH) · evening of results day
ALL: (1) results OUT (never pre-results — that's gambling); (2) genuine beat: EPS above broker
preview (or strong YoY + QoQ + dividend surprise); revenue/profit both up; (3) stock closed
announcement day UP on ≥1.5x volume; (4) was above 50MA or top-third RS pre-results; (5)
regime FULL/CAUTION. Action: WATCHLIST for Day 1–2 continuation. Pre-write: entry = close
above announcement-day high; stop = below announcement-day low; T1 +8%. If circuit-locked:
NO chase orders — wait for tradeable day.
STAGE 2 — BUY TRIGGER · Window A, Day 1–2 after results
ALL: (1) Day 1 or 2 after announcement; (2) price trades/closes ABOVE announcement-day high
after 10:00 AM; (3) up-volume still ≥ average; (4) not more than +6% above announcement close
(avoid chasing); (5) index not dumping that morning. Order: limit at day-high +0.3%. Stop same
session. Reasoning: Day 1–2 continuation proves the beat is being ACCUMULATED, not sold into;
the 10 AM rule skips the emotional open; the +6% cap keeps R:R intact — drift works from fair
prices, not extended ones.
Trade frame
Stop below announcement-day low (~3–5% under entry) · ½ at +8%, trail 20MA · 2–6 weeks ·
dead after 3 weeks → exit.
Invalidation: closes back below announcement-day low · volume
vanishes (<0.7x avg for 3 days) · sector turns red.
Example
UBL beats + dividend: day 250→262 (2.5x vol). Next day closes 266 above 262 → BUY 266. Stop
240 (risk 26). T1 287. Rs 5k = 192 sh.
Don'ts
Never hold INTO results; never buy one-off-gain "beats"; never chase circuit locks.

L05 — M2: 52-Week High [1% · standard · Window A/B]
Edge: anchoring bias — investors underreact at old highs, creating sustained drift; academic
momentum's most robust signal. Best when: FULL regime strictly. Downtrend rule: HARD OFF
in CAUTION/DEFENSE — new-high breakouts in weak markets are bull traps >60% of the time.
When market turns down, existing M2 runners get trailed tight (below 10MA).
STAGE 1 — PRE-SIGNAL (WATCH) · evening scan
ALL: (1) within 5% of 52-week high; (2) basing under it 1–4 weeks (flat, not vertical); (3) KSE-100
above rising 50MA; (4) not >10% above last base in <2 weeks; (5) large-cap liquid only; (6) no
announcements 48h. Action: WATCHLIST. Pre-write: breakout line = 52wk high; stop 4.5% below
it; T1 +8%. Alert AT the high.
STAGE 2 — BUY TRIGGER · Window A next morning (or retest)
Path 1: daily CLOSE ≥1–2% above high on ≥1.5x volume → BUY next morning Window A (limit at
close +0.5%, only if open < +2% above close). Path 2 (preferred): first pullback holding above
old high + bullish candle → BUY above its high. Order: limit, chase-capped. Stop same session.
Reasoning: the volume close proves supply at the historic high is absorbed; blue-sky breakouts
have no overhead sellers, so measured follow-through is the highest-probability drift in the book
— but ONLY with a healthy index underneath.
Trade frame
Stop 4–5% below old high · ½ at +8%, trail 20MA (or weekly Gann 1x1) · 3–8 weeks.
Invalidation: close back below old high · breakout volume <1.2x · index
loses 50MA (tighten to breakeven immediately).
Example
LUCK bases 970–995 under 1000, closes 1015 (2x) → BUY 1018. Stop 970 (risk 48). T1 1100. Rs 5k
= 104 sh.
Don'ts
Never buy 8–12% above the break; never trade illiquid new-high spikes.

L06 — C1: Bull Flag & Pennant [1% · standard · Window
A/B]
Edge: pole = institutional urgency; tight flag = no distribution; second leg mirrors the first. Best
when: FULL regime, fresh catalyst (results/news). Downtrend rule: OFF in DEFENSE — flags in
falling markets break DOWN (that's SC1's market).
STAGE 1 — PRE-SIGNAL (WATCH) · evening scan
ALL: (1) pole +10–20% in 1–5 days on 2x+ volume; (2) now consolidating 3–10 days, drifting
down/sideways (flag) or triangle (pennant); (3) flag volume visibly dying (<0.8x avg); (4) holding
ABOVE pole midpoint; (5) flag age <15 sessions; (6) FULL regime. Action: WATCHLIST. Pre-write:
flag top/bottom lines, stop 2% below flag low, target = pole length added. Alert at flag top.
STAGE 2 — BUY TRIGGER · Window A or B
ALL: (1) CLOSE above flag's upper edge; (2) volume expanding (≥1.3x); (3) close in top half of
range; (4) still within 6% of flag (not vertical already). Order: limit at break +0.3%. Stop same
session. Reasoning: dying volume in the flag proves profit-taking is exhausted WITHOUT supply
appearing; the volume break proves the next accumulation wave started. Half of flags never
break — the close-above-edge rule is what filters them.
Trade frame
Stop ~2% below flag low · ½ fast at +6–8%, tight trail (10/20MA) · 1–3 weeks (sprinters).
Invalidation: close below flag low · flag ages past 15 sessions (reclassify
as range or drop) · pole midpoint breaks.
Example
POL 500→570 pole, flag 560–570 (6 days, dying vol) → closes 574 (2x) → BUY 575. Stop 548 (risk
27). Target 644. Rs 5k = 185 sh.
Don'ts
Never buy inside the flag; never accept flags below pole midpoint.

L07 — C2: Cup & Handle [1% · standard · Window A/B]
Edge: months of base-building absorbs ALL supply; handle shakes the last weak hands; rim
break = cleanest institutional entry. Best when: FULL regime, large-caps. Downtrend rule: OFF
in DEFENSE — cups complete downward in bear markets; handles deepen into failures.
STAGE 1 — PRE-SIGNAL (WATCH) · evening scan (weekly + daily)
ALL: (1) U-shape (rounded, not V) over 1–6 months, depth 10–25%; (2) price back within 5% of
left rim; (3) handle forming: 1–3 week drift −5–12% on LOW volume, in UPPER HALF of cup; (4)
uptrend existed BEFORE the cup; (5) FULL regime. Action: WATCHLIST (this one develops slowly
— check weekly). Pre-write: rim line, handle low, stop 2.5% below handle low, target = cup depth.
Alert at rim.
STAGE 2 — BUY TRIGGER · Window A/B
ALL: (1) CLOSE above rim/handle-high; (2) volume ≥1.5x; (3) close top-half; (4) handle intact
(never broke below its low). Order: limit at rim +0.5%. Stop same session. Reasoning: the U
proves gradual absorption (V = panic, no base); the shallow low-volume handle proves remaining
sellers are trivial; rim-break volume proves institutions are done accumulating and starting
markup.
Trade frame
Stop 2–3% below handle low · ½ at +8–10%, trail 20MA/weekly 1x1 · 1–3 months (longest swing).
Invalidation: close back below handle low · rim break on <1.2x volume
(fake) · weekly trend breaks.
Example
MEBL rim 250, base 210, handle to 240 (low vol) → closes 253 (1.8x) → BUY 254. Stop 233 (risk
21). Target 293. Rs 5k = 238 sh.
Don'ts
Never trade V-shapes as cups; never accept lower-half handles; never force on small-caps.

L08 — C3: Ascending Triangle [1% · standard · Window
A/B]
Edge: flat top + rising lows = buyers paying up repeatedly into fixed supply; coiled spring with
visible pressure gauge. Best when: FULL regime. Downtrend rule: OFF in DEFENSE/CAUTION
— triangles in weak markets break down (support gives way).
STAGE 1 — PRE-SIGNAL (WATCH) · evening scan
ALL: (1) flat resistance, 2–3+ touches, 3+ weeks; (2) clearly RISING lows (each dip bought
sooner); (3) within 3% of ceiling; (4) volume fading into squeeze; (5) FULL regime; (6) no
announcements 48h. Action: WATCHLIST. Pre-write: ceiling, rising trendline, stop below last
higher low, target = back-height added. Alert at ceiling.
STAGE 2 — BUY TRIGGER · Window A/B
ALL: (1) CLOSE 1–2% above flat top on ≥1.5x volume; (2) preferred: retest-hold (dip to ceiling
±1% + bullish candle) → BUY above its high; (3) close top-half. Order: limit. Stop same session.
Reasoning: rising lows prove demand strengthening INTO supply; the volume break proves the
ceiling's sellers are exhausted. Identical mechanics to L02 with an extra demand-pressure read =
higher confidence, same discipline.
Trade frame
Stop ~2–3% below last higher low/trendline (down-break = INSTANT exit) · ½ at +7–8%, trail ·
2–6 weeks.
Invalidation: close below rising trendline · volume-less break · regime
flip.
Example
EFERT ceiling 200 (3 touches), lows 180→186→192 → closes 203 (2x) → BUY 204. Stop 187 (risk
17). Target 223. Rs 5k = 294 sh.
Don'ts
Never enter pre-break (rising wedges look identical until they break DOWN).

L09 — R1: Double / Triple Bottom [0.5% ADV · Window
A/B]
Edge: two/three demand prints at one floor + neckline break = most evidence-backed bullish
reversal (65–88% across studies when confirmed). Best when: CAUTION-to-stabilizing market,
AFTER washout. Downtrend rule: STANDBY in active DEFENSE (falling knife freezes all bottom
entries) — allowed ONLY after index stabilization signal (hammer/calm + holds washout low).
Never bottom-fish crash day 1–3.
STAGE 1 — PRE-SIGNAL (WATCH) · evening scan
ALL: (1) clear 2+ week downtrend INTO bottom #1; (2) two bottoms within 2–3% (triple: three),
10+ days apart; (3) 2nd bottom on lighter/equal volume with wick rejection (hammer); (4)
neckline = inter-bottom peak drawn; (5) RSI washed <35 then flattening; (6) index stabilizing or
better. Action: WATCHLIST. Pre-write: neckline, stop 2.5% below lowest bottom, target =
measured move. Alert at neckline. DO NOT BUY the bottom — wait for the neck.
STAGE 2 — BUY TRIGGER · Window A/B
ALL: (1) CLOSE 2%+ above neckline on ≥1.5x volume; (2) preferred: retest-hold of neckline +
bullish candle → BUY above its high; (3) close top-half; (4) index not printing new lows that day.
Order: limit. Stop same session. Reasoning: bottoms alone are guesses — the neckline BREAK
proves sellers lost control of the range (structure shift); volume proves institutional participation,
not short-covering noise; the retest variant adds the L02 double-confirmation.
Trade frame
Stop 2–3% below lowest bottom · ½ at +7–8%, trail · 2–6 weeks.
Invalidation: break-then-collapse back below neckline (bull trap — honor
stop) · 2nd bottom volume heavier than 1st (skip at Stage 1).
Example
PPL: 150 → 170 neck → 152 (lighter vol, hammer) → closes 174 (2x) → BUY 175. Stop 147 (risk 28).
T1 188. Rs 2.5k = 89 sh.
Don'ts
Never buy bottom #2 pre-break ("forming double bottom" = prediction, not signal).

L10 — R2: Spring / Failed Breakdown [0.5% ADV · Window
A]
Edge: stop-hunt below support traps sellers; their forced buyback + reclaim buyers = fuel.
Tightest natural stop in the book. Best when: stabilizing/CAUTION market, quality levels.
Downtrend rule: STANDBY in active DEFENSE — springs fail in crashes (dip keeps dipping). Wait
for index stabilization day.
STAGE 1 — PRE-SIGNAL (WATCH) · evening scan
ALL: (1) support held 2+ times with REAL bounces; (2) price now within 2% ABOVE support and
soft; (3) prior bounces each ≥5%; (4) index stabilizing/flat (not dumping); (5) no bad-news reason
for breakdown. Action: WATCHLIST for the dip-and-reclaim sequence. Pre-write: support line,
"dip zone" 1–3% below, reclaim rule (CLOSE back above), stop 1.5% below dip low (set AFTER dip
prints). Alert 0.5% below support.
STAGE 2 — BUY TRIGGER · Window A (day after reclaim)
ALL: (1) dip 1–3% below support printed; (2) CLOSE back ABOVE support within 1–3 days on
≥1.5x volume; (3) next morning trades above reclaim candle's high after 10:00 AM (or
pullback-holds above support + bullish candle); (4) index not breaking down that morning.
Order: buy-stop/limit at reclaim-high +0.3%. Stop same session. Reasoning: the dip flushed
weak longs and pulled in breakdown shorts; the HIGH-VOLUME reclaim close proves a bigger
buyer absorbed BOTH groups; entering above the reclaim high (not during the dip) means
momentum + trapped shorts both push your way.
Trade frame
Stop 1–2% below dip low · T1 mid-range = ½, then prior resistance · breakeven stop after T1.
Invalidation: 4+ days stuck below support (real breakdown — cancel) ·
reclaim on weak volume · close back below support post-entry.
Example
HBL 160 support → dips 156 → closes 163 (2x) → next day above 165 → BUY 165. Stop 154 (risk
11). T1 174, T2 182. Rs 2.5k = 227 sh.
Don'ts
Never buy DURING the dip ("discount"); never widen the tight stop — it IS the edge.

L11 — R3: RSI Bullish Divergence [0.5% ADV · Window A]
Edge: momentum exhaustion made visible — sellers push price lower on fading energy; the
confirmation candle proves buyers stepped in. Best when: stabilizing market at known support.
Downtrend rule: STANDBY in active DEFENSE — divergence persists for weeks in crashes
(repeat lower lows). Needs stabilization + support confluence.
STAGE 1 — PRE-SIGNAL (WATCH) · evening scan
ALL: (1) downtrend into KNOWN support (prior lows/round/200MA zone); (2) RSI(14) <35 and
flattening; (3) two swing lows forming where 2nd is lower in price (divergence developing — RSI
higher low); (4) DAILY chart; (5) index stabilizing. Action: WATCHLIST. Pre-write: support,
divergence pair levels, stop 2% below confirmation-candle low (set at trigger), target = 20/50MA.
