ℹ️ How to use this tab — Backtest
  1. Pick a symbol (start with OGDC/HBL/LUCK/FFC/MEBL), a strategy, and dates (use 2–3+ years).
  2. Set capital and ATR stop (2.0 = normal; lower = tighter stop), then Run backtest.
  3. Read honestly: Total return vs Buy & hold · Sharpe (want >1) · Max drawdown (can you survive it?) · Profit factor (must be >1.0) · equity curve + trade list.

How it works: the engine replays history day-by-day, filling orders at the next open with commission + slippage, guarded by an ATR stop. Past profit never guarantees future profit — backtests exist to kill bad ideas.

ℹ️ How to use this tab — Trading
  1. The 🏦 Real Broker Account panel tracks your actual trades (account W615), with live market prices powering every P&L figure.
  2. Add a trade: symbol + BUY/SELL + qty. Leave rate blank to fill at the live price. Statement trades came with commission included; manual trades are booked at qty × rate.
  3. Close a position: press the red ✕ Close button on any open row (asks how many shares; blank = all) and it sells at the live price.
  4. 🤖 Ask AI: sends your live portfolio + recent trades to your configured AI engine for an honest assessment and management suggestions (no key → rule-based notes).
  5. 📤 Update from a broker statement: expand the section at the bottom, upload your statement PDF, check the detected positions in the preview (editable), then Apply — the book is replaced with those positions.
  6. The book is saved in data/broker_account.json. Reset to statement restores the original W615 trades.

Paper trading (fake money, practice) is still available further down, collapsed by default.

ℹ️ How to use this tab — Swing Money Management
  1. Set your swing plan once: capital (Rs), risk % per trade, target % per trade, stop %, max open positions, max hold days, weekly loss limit.
  2. Before every swing use the Position Size calculator — pick your stock, entry, stop & target, and it gives the exact shares to buy (rounded to PSX 100-share lots).
  3. After every exit log it in the Swing Journal — it tracks hold time, % gain, R-multiples, win rate, and weekly losses.
  4. 📄 Trade Plan PDF: pick any stock and download one PDF with ALL its research, the best-fit strategy (all 33 backtested on it), and a trade plan sized by YOUR rules.
  5. Never add to a loser. The martingale ladder at the bottom is an education demo of why averaging down blows swing accounts — it is NOT a strategy.
